Key Features Across All Models

  • Universal Compatibility: Works with 60Hz power systems (Korean standard)
  • Custom Design Service: Available for other frequencies (50Hz, etc.) and international markets
  • Wide Voltage Range: Compatible with 220V, 380V, 440V, and 480V systems
  • High Efficiency: All models achieve power factor of 0.95 or higher
  • Compact Design: Space-efficient installation for various facility layouts
  • Easy Installation: Plug-and-play setup with minimal downtime
  • Global Market Ready: Custom specifications for different countries and power systems

Korea Electric Power Corporation Power Factor Rate System Changes (Effective February 1, 2025)

⚠️ Major Changes

Standard Power Factor Increase: Average lagging power factor 90% → 92% upward adjustment

Power Factor Application Time Change: Lagging power factor 09:00-23:00 → 08:00-22:00, Leading power factor 23:00-09:00 → 22:00-08:00

Power Factor Rate Adjustment: Surcharge and discount rates apply based on power factor performance
