Welcome to KESA Korea Electric Saving Association, your trusted partner in power saving solutions and sustainable energy management.

Company Vision

At KESA, we envision a world where businesses can achieve both economic growth and environmental sustainability. Our mission is to provide cutting-edge power saving technology that helps companies reduce electricity costs while contributing to global carbon neutrality goals.

We believe that energy efficiency is not just about cost savings—it's about creating a better future for generations to come. Through our innovative KESA Series products, we're making this vision a reality.

Core Values

Innovation: We continuously research and develop new technologies to provide the most effective power saving solutions in the market.

Quality: Every product we manufacture meets the highest international standards, ensuring reliability and performance for our customers.

Sustainability: We're committed to environmental responsibility, helping businesses reduce their carbon footprint while improving their bottom line.

Partnership: We work closely with our clients to understand their unique needs and provide customized solutions that deliver measurable results.

Our Commitment

KESA is dedicated to helping businesses achieve 10%+ electricity cost savings through our advanced power saving technology. Our solutions not only reduce energy consumption but also improve power quality, leading to enhanced equipment performance and productivity.

As we move toward the 2050 carbon-neutral society, KESA stands ready to support your company's sustainability journey with proven, reliable technology and expert consultation services.
