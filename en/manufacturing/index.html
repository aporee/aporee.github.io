Manufacturing Excellence

KESA power saving devices are manufactured in our state-of-the-art facility in Busan, South Korea, using the latest technology and strict quality control processes.

Our manufacturing facility is equipped with advanced automation systems and precision testing equipment to ensure every product meets the highest international standards for safety and performance.

Core Manufacturing Capabilities

Advanced Electronics

  • Surface mount technology (SMT) assembly
  • Automated component placement
  • Precision soldering and quality testing
  • Environmental stress testing

Quality Assurance

  • ISO 9001 certified quality management
  • 100% functional testing
  • Environmental compliance testing
  • Long-term reliability validation

Facility & Infrastructure

Our manufacturing facility covers over 2,000 square meters and includes dedicated areas for:

  • Component storage and management
  • Assembly and testing
  • Quality control and inspection
  • Research and development
  • Customer service and support

The facility operates under strict environmental controls to ensure consistent product quality and reliability across all production batches.

Technology & Innovation

R&D Investment

We invest heavily in research and development to continuously improve our power saving technology. Our engineering team works closely with leading universities and research institutions to develop next-generation solutions.

Recent innovations include advanced harmonic filtering algorithms, real-time power quality monitoring, and AI-powered optimization systems that automatically adjust device parameters for maximum efficiency.

Patent Portfolio

KESA holds multiple patents for our unique power saving technology, including:

  • Adaptive power factor correction
  • Intelligent harmonic suppression
  • Dynamic load balancing
  • Predictive maintenance algorithms
